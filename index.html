Available Sessions

All sessions are led by a fully qualified and experienced primary teacher with Forest School and Wild Beach Leader level 3 certification. We are experienced in supporting children with SEND. Our leader is also a trained outdoor first aider with paediatrics, food hygiene and safety, who holds public liability insurance and DBS. All activities are fully risk assessed.

Winter Forest School

Monday Drop in Sessions 11am-2pm

Ideal for 18 months to 11 year olds, with a designated 'calm hour' ideal for younger ones and children with SEN from 11-12. Join us for nature play, den building, obstacle courses, scavenger hunts, cooking around the campfire, and more.
